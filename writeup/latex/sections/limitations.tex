\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,topsep=0.2em,itemsep=0.2em]
  \item The ranked configuration is inferred from its listed runtime and resubmission
        date. The judged commit, its coordinates and the device that ran its
        single-precision descent were not recorded.
  \item The \reproduction{} result is one run on one host, on a CPU rather than the
        judges' GPU. Coordinate descent is capped by wall time, so its scores vary
        from run to run.
  \item \historical{} records differ in code, hardware, budget and concurrency, and
        most have no saved coordinates. Their component shares are arithmetic, not
        causal.
  \item By the same inference, Lanes B and C were never scored. The curvature
        evidence comes from uncapped runs without controls, and 12 of its 34 saved
        endpoints extend past a canvas edge by one single-precision rounding step
        (\measurement).
  \item Speed and error figures come from 14 moves on two designs through the
        single-move path, so the acceptance margin holds only on the sampled states.
  \item The smooth score keeps kinks from top-$k$ selection and clamps, undercounts
        density at the canvas edge, and star-routes three-pin nets.
  \item Development reused the 17 public designs that set the ranking, and each
        competition design ran once. We report no place-and-route results (timing,
        power, area), and real flows discard the soft clusters that we place to lower
        the proxy.
\end{itemize}
