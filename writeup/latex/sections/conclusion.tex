\section{Conclusion and future work}\label{sec:conclusion}

\inbrief{Smooth proposals with exact acceptance placed ninth in a listed 12 minutes
per design; a reproduction of the ranked configuration and a controlled comparison
are the next steps.}

Thinkorplace splits macro placement into smooth global proposals and exact,
incremental local acceptance, and placed ninth on the final leaderboard with a
verified mean proxy of 0.9974 in a listed 12 minutes per design (\official). The
development records add three lessons: the score is mostly congestion, and so was
most of our improvement; a fast pipeline from a good start beat close to an hour of
elaborate search; and curvature kicks must be judged against cheaper perturbations.
The first next step is an end-to-end reproduction of the ranked configuration on all
17 designs, once the checkpoint defect it exposed is fixed. The open questions then
need a controlled study of v1, the single pipeline and the three-lane system on the
17 designs under matched hardware, budget and aggregate compute (51 runs with saved
coordinates), with random-direction and zero-step controls for the curvature kicks, a
net-disjoint control for assignment, and place-and-route checks.
