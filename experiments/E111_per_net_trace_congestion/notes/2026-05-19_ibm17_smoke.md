## Why the existing smooth proxy was wrong

Canonical `PlacementCost.get_routing` (plc_client_os.py:1514) traces an
L-route per (source, sink) pair:

  - H demand on cells (source_row, c) for c in range(min(s_col, k_col), max(s_col, k_col))
  - V demand on cells (r, sink_col) for r in range(min(s_row, k_row), max(s_row, k_row))
  - Hard macros add x_dist × vroute_alloc to V_macro and y_dist × hroute_alloc to H_macro per footprint cell.
  - Then normalize by route capacity, ±smooth_range box-smooth, sum (macro + nets), top-5% mean of concat(V, H).

The DPO-era `_rudy_congestion` (ablation_v2_steps.py:505) spreads each
net's weight UNIFORMLY across its bbox. That over-allocates demand to
cells the canonical never routes through — every internal cell of the
bbox eats demand under bbox-uniform, while canonical only touches the
source-row strip and sink-column strip.

On ibm17 cached cascade plateau:
  - canonical:    1.895
  - bbox (E88):   6.815  (+260% off)
  - **trace (E111): 2.167  (+14.4% off)**

Δcong correlation across 16 random 5-macro perturbations:
  - bbox: Pearson 0.52, Spearman 0.19, sign-agree 44%
  - **trace: Pearson 0.59, Spearman 0.59, sign-agree 56%**

## Algorithm sketch

For each (source, sink) pair:
  1. Soft cell assignment: row_src[p, r] = softmax_r(-((src_y - cell_y_ctr[r]) / σ)²) with σ = 0.5 × cell_h. Same for col_src, row_snk, col_snk.
  2. Soft expected cell index: row_src_exp[p] = Σ_r row_src[p, r] · r. Same for the other three.
  3. Smooth (row_min, row_max), (col_min, col_max) via logsumexp with β = 6.
  4. Range indicator I(c) ≈ 1 for c ∈ {col_min .. col_max - 1}:
     I(c) = sigmoid(β·(c - col_min + 0.5)) - sigmoid(β·(c - col_max + 0.5))
  5. H_route[r, c] += sum_p w_p · row_src[p, r] · h_col_range[p, c]
     V_route[r, c] += sum_p w_p · v_row_range[p, r] · col_snk[p, c]
  6. Chunked: process pair_chunk_size pairs at a time, contract via matmul
     to avoid [B, gr, gc] materialization.

Hard macros add their footprint contribution via clamp-overlap × routing_alloc.

±smooth_range box smoothing: separable 1-D conv on each axis with
edge-active-cell-count normalization to exactly match canonical's
boundary clipping.

Combine: V_total + H_total, concat-flatten, top-5% mean.

## Risks

- M3 run-to-run variance ~0.3-0.5%, so single-bench numbers > 0.5%
  delta are meaningful, but smaller deltas are noise.
- Hardware variance (M3 → EPYC) is ~2.4%, so ship-day judges' EPYC
  numbers will be different.
- Only one bench (ibm17) tested end-to-end so far. Could be a fluke
  before ibm10/12/ariane133 confirm.
